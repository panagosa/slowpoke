about

LaVar James is a writer/producer based in NY and LA. He is currently producing The Pete Davidson show on Netflix with Above Average. Previous work includes managing the nationwide tour of Turbo Fonzarelli as well Pete Davidson’s current US and international comedy tours. He has also worked on creative projects with Crocs, doublesoul, AXE, Reformation, Verizon, and other brand partners.
